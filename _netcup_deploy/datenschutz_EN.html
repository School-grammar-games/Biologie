Privacy Policy

This notice briefly explains which data may be processed when EduBioSim is visited.

Provider and contact details are available in the legal notice.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by netcup. When pages are accessed, the web server may process technical access data, for example IP address, date and time of access, requested file, transferred data volume, browser information, and status code. This data is used to operate the website securely and reliably.

Cookies

EduBioSim does not use its own cookies for advertising or tracking purposes. For this reason, no cookie banner is used.

Web analytics with GoatCounter

GoatCounter is used for privacy-friendly reach measurement. According to GoatCounter, it does not use cookies, LocalStorage, or persistent browser identifiers. Aggregated statistics are collected to understand which pages and simulations are used.

The analysis does not involve cross-site tracking or personalized advertising. Further information is provided by GoatCounter at goatcounter.com/help/privacy and goatcounter.com/help/gdpr.
